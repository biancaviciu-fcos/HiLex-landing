HiLex Membership Terms and Conditions

Essential and Premium individual memberships

Forest & Co Solicitors

1 Introduction And Purpose

1.1 These Terms govern the HiLex individual Membership. HiLex is a legal education and membership platform operated by Forest Corporate Ltd, trading as Forest & Co ('the Firm', 'we', 'our', 'us'). The Firm is the contractual provider, receives Membership payments, acts as Data Controller and provides the legal consultations. Forest Corporate Ltd is registered in England and Wales (company number 11229601) and authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA number 647302).

Contact: enquiries@fcos.co.uk; Forest & Co, 16 Berkeley Street, London, W1J 8DZ, United Kingdom.

1.2 The Membership is a legal guidance service designed primarily for Romanian families residing in the United Kingdom. It provides clarity, practical legal guidance, and support for real-life personal legal situations.

1.3 The Membership does not include full legal representation, unlimited legal advice, or business-related legal services. It is intended to provide Members with actionable guidance aligned with the ethos: "We do not explain the law. We explain what you need to do."

2 Definitions

The following definitions apply throughout these Terms:

2.1 "HiLex Individuals Essential" refers to the Membership level priced at £180 + VAT annually, as set out in Clause 5.

2.2 "HiLex Individuals Premium" refers to the Membership level priced at £280 + VAT annually, as set out in Clause 5.

2.3 "Consultation" means a session with a legal professional from the Firm, using the annual credit available under the Member’s package.

2.4 "Annual Credit" means the total time included in a Membership for legal consultations or legal assistance: up to 30 minutes per year for Essential and up to 60 minutes per year in total for Premium.

2.5 "Member-only Educational Materials" includes videos, PDFs, checklists, and other learning resources restricted to Membership use.

2.6 "Selected Legal Services" means other services provided by the Firm that are unrelated to the Membership and eligible for discounts in accordance with these Terms.

3 Eligibility And Restrictions

3.1 Membership is available to individuals only and applies exclusively to personal legal matters. Legal services pertaining to businesses, companies, commercial entities, or corporate situations are not included in this Membership.

3.2 The Membership is intended for individual use. However, discussions through Consultations may include matters involving family members or close persons where directly relevant.

3.3 The Firm reserves the right to refuse or cancel Membership or related services under Clause 13 where:

A conflict of interest is present;

The Member engages in abusive behaviour or misconduct;

External regulatory or ethical considerations prohibit the Firm from acting; or

The matter is outside the scope of services provided by the Firm.

3.4 For the avoidance of doubt, the Membership is strictly for individuals and is not destined for businesses. It is not available to companies, sole traders, partnerships, or any other commercial or corporate entity.

4 Membership Duration

4.1 Membership commences upon receipt of full payment and activation by the Firm and remains valid for a period of 12 months. Renewal is subject to the terms prevailing at the time of renewal.

4.2 Members must utilise all included Annual Credit and benefits within the Membership period. Unused credit and benefits expire at the end of that period and are non-transferable to a future term.

4.3 For gift Memberships, activation occurs when the recipient redeems the Membership, subject to eligibility under Clause 3. Gift Memberships must be redeemed within 12 months of purchase and are non-transferable. Any cancellation or refund is subject to Clause 9 and applicable statutory rights.

5 Membership Benefits And Exclusions

5.1 HiLex Individuals Essential (£180 + VAT per year):

Up to 30 minutes of Annual Credit for legal consultations or focused legal assistance.

Access to the HiLex Essential platform, including informative materials and practical guides for life in the United Kingdom, for 12 months from activation.

Response to enquiries within 24 hours.

Preferential discounts on Selected Legal Services provided by Forest & Co.

5.2 HiLex Individuals Premium (£280 + VAT per year) includes all Essential benefits, plus:

Up to 60 minutes of Annual Credit in total for legal consultations or more in-depth legal assistance. This total includes, and is not in addition to, the 30 minutes included in Essential.

Access to the separate HiLex Premium platform, with exclusive video materials and resources, for the Membership period.

Response to enquiries within 12 hours.

Priority in communication and responses.

Enhanced preferential discounts on Selected Legal Services provided by Forest & Co.

5.3 Exclusions:

Membership does not include full legal representation, complex document drafting, litigation, court work, or ongoing case management.

Business-related legal services are specifically excluded and addressed separately by the Firm.

Consultations and legal assistance covered by Annual Credit may not exceed the total time included in the Member’s package unless expressly agreed otherwise.

No standalone document review is included as a separate Membership benefit. Any document review or revision outside the agreed use of Annual Credit will be separately invoiced.

6 Booking And Consultations

6.1 Consultations must be booked in advance and are subject to availability. Members may contact the Firm by telephone and/or email for scheduling.

6.2 Consultations may be provided online or in person, depending on availability and suitability as determined by the Firm.

6.3 Members must provide a minimum of 24 hours notice if cancelling or rescheduling a Consultation. Otherwise, the Firm reserves the right to treat the Consultation as used.

6.4 Consultations will be conducted by any fee earner of the Firm who is specialised in the practice area relevant to the Member’s legal matter.

6.5 Consultations provide general legal guidance only. Where full legal advice, representation or ongoing casework is required, a separate retainer and client care documentation will be agreed. The scope of any assistance using Annual Credit will be confirmed by the Firm; Membership does not itself create an ongoing retainer for a particular matter.

7 Member-Only Educational Materials

7.1 Members are granted access to Educational Materials according to their package. Essential includes access to the HiLex Essential platform; Premium includes Essential access and the separate HiLex Premium platform with exclusive materials and resources. These materials are for personal, non-commercial use only and must not be shared, copied, or distributed to third parties.

7.2 Important information about educational materials. Videos, guides, PDFs and other resources provide general educational information, not advice tailored to a particular case. They may become outdated as legislation or case law changes. Members should obtain guidance appropriate to their circumstances before acting on them.
