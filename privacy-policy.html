Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how we collect and use personal information when you visit HiLex, become a member, contact us or subscribe to our updates. It also explains your choices, your rights and how to contact us.

1 Who we are

HiLex is a legal education and membership platform operated by Forest Corporate Ltd, trading as Forest & Co. Forest Corporate Ltd is the controller responsible for the personal information described in this policy. It is registered in England and Wales under company number 11229601 and authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority under SRA number 647302.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email enquiries@fcos.co.uk or write to Forest & Co, 16 Berkeley Street, London, W1J 8DZ, United Kingdom.

This policy covers visitors to hilex.co.uk, HiLex members, people making enquiries or booking membership consultations, and newsletter subscribers. If you instruct the Firm on a separate legal matter, additional privacy information and client care terms may apply to that work.

2 Information we collect

Account and contact information: your name, email address, telephone number, login credentials and account identifiers; postal or billing details where needed; and your communication preferences.

Membership and payment information: your selected package, membership status and dates, payment and subscription references, billing records, refunds and the use of your included consultation credit. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; HiLex does not store your full card number or card security code.

Communications and consultation information: enquiries, messages, booking details and information you choose to give us about your circumstances. Depending on your enquiry, this may include sensitive information, such as health information, or information about criminal allegations or proceedings. Please only send information relevant to your enquiry and avoid including unnecessary information about other people.

Platform and technical information: IP address, browser and device information, authentication and security logs, pages or resources accessed, video or lesson activity, saved resources and completion progress. These records support access to the service, your learning progress and platform security.

Newsletter information: your name and email address where provided, subscription preferences, consent records and unsubscribe requests. Our newsletter service also processes delivery and interaction information where that functionality is enabled and legally permitted.

We receive information directly from you, through your use of the website or platform, and from providers handling payments, bookings, authentication and delivery of our services. If someone buys a gift membership or communicates on your behalf, they may provide your contact details. We will provide privacy information when we contact you, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

3 Why we use information and our lawful bases

Contract and steps requested before a contract: to set up your account, provide the membership and resources you purchase, process payments, manage bookings and included credit, respond to service requests, and send essential membership communications. Without the information needed for these activities, we may be unable to provide the relevant service.

Legitimate interests: to answer general enquiries, administer and improve the platform, maintain security, prevent misuse or fraud, keep appropriate business records, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Our interests are in operating a reliable service and protecting our business and users; we consider your interests and rights before relying on this basis. You may object as explained below.

Legal obligations: to meet applicable accounting, tax, regulatory and professional requirements, and respond to lawful requests from competent authorities.

Consent: where required for newsletters or other electronic marketing, optional cookies or similar tracking, and any other activity for which we specifically ask your permission. Consent is separate from accepting membership terms and can be withdrawn at any time without affecting processing already carried out lawfully.

Where an enquiry or legal service requires special category information, we also need an applicable condition under Article 9 of the UK GDPR, such as necessity for establishing, exercising or defending legal claims, or explicit consent where appropriate. Criminal offence information is handled only where permitted by Article 10 and the Data Protection Act 2018. We will explain any additional basis or safeguards relevant to a particular service when needed.

4 Newsletters and marketing

We use a newsletter service provider to manage subscriptions and send updates about HiLex content, membership and relevant services. Electronic marketing is sent with your consent where required. Where the law permits marketing about our own similar services without separate consent, we provide an opt-out when collecting your details and in subsequent messages.

You can unsubscribe through the link in a marketing email or by contacting enquiries@fcos.co.uk. We may retain a minimal suppression record to respect your choice. Unsubscribing does not stop essential account, payment, security or service messages.

If email tracking is used, it may record email opens or link interactions. Where consent is required for that tracking, it must be obtained separately from any permission that does not cover it. You can ask us about the tracking used in the communications you receive.

5 Communications and consultation tools

If you contact us through WhatsApp, your telephone number, profile details and messages are processed to respond to you. WhatsApp also processes information under its own privacy terms. You may choose email instead; please ask us for an appropriate channel before sending particularly sensitive documents.

Where a consultation takes place through a video meeting service, such as Zoom or Microsoft Teams, the provider processes information needed to connect the call, including participant details, audio and video you enable, and technical connection information. The invitation identifies the service used; its own privacy information may also apply.

This policy does not itself authorise recording or transcription of consultations. If recording or transcription is proposed, we will explain in advance its purpose, lawful basis, who will have access and how long the record will be retained, and obtain consent where required. Any AI-assisted transcription would be covered by that additional information.

6 Limited use of AI tools

We may use AI-assisted software on a limited basis in supporting our work. Such tools may help prepare or organise content and administrative material; they do not replace the professional judgment of the people responsible for our services.

Any use involving personal information must have a defined purpose and lawful basis, use only the information necessary, and be subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and supplier safeguards. This policy is not blanket consent to upload consultation records or sensitive information to AI systems, or to use them to train general-purpose AI models. If a proposed use changes how your information is handled, we will provide the relevant information and obtain consent where required before that use.

HiLex membership decisions and legal guidance are not determined solely by automated AI decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on you. You can contact us with questions about a particular use of AI affecting your information.

7 Who receives information

Information is accessible to authorised staff and professionals within the Firm who need it for their work. We also use service providers for website hosting, account authentication, databases and file storage, video delivery, payment processing, transactional email, newsletters, booking and communications, IT support and security. They receive the information needed for the relevant service.

Providers processing information on our behalf must act under appropriate contractual arrangements. Some providers, including payment or communication services, may also act as independent controllers for their own legal, security or service purposes and explain this in their privacy notices. You can request more information about providers relevant to your use of HiLex.

We may disclose information to professional advisers, insurers, regulators, courts, law enforcement or other authorities where required or lawfully justified. Information relevant to a separate legal matter may be shared as explained in that matter's privacy and client care information. We do not sell your personal information.

8 International transfers

Some service providers may store or access information outside the United Kingdom. Where this involves a restricted transfer, we must use an applicable UK adequacy arrangement or appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses, together with any assessment or supplementary measures required by law.

You can contact enquiries@fcos.co.uk for information about relevant destinations and safeguards, including a copy of applicable safeguards with confidential details removed where necessary.

9 Cookies and similar technologies

The member platform uses cookies and similar storage necessary for authentication, security and session management. A remember-me option may keep you signed in for longer when selected. Your browser or device may also cache resources to help the service operate.

The public landing page is not currently configured with advertising trackers or a third-party analytics script. Hosting and security providers may nevertheless process technical request information. Images or other content loaded from external providers may expose your IP address and browser information to those providers when requested.

If optional analytics, advertising cookies or other technologies requiring consent are introduced, they must remain disabled until the required consent has been obtained. A cookie notice or settings control will explain their purposes and how to change your choice. You can also manage cookies through your browser; blocking essential cookies may prevent login or other functions from working.

10 How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for its purpose and applicable legal or professional obligations. Retention depends on the type of record, whether your membership or enquiry remains active, outstanding payments or complaints, relevant statutory requirements and the period during which a legal claim may arise.

Account and activity information is retained to operate your membership and for a justified period afterwards for account queries, security or disputes. Billing and transaction records are kept for the applicable accounting and tax record-keeping period. Enquiry and consultation records are retained according to their subject, professional obligations and any associated claim or matter. A separate legal matter may have its own retention arrangements.

Newsletter details are retained while your subscription remains active or there is another lawful basis to contact you, with minimal suppression information retained where needed to honour an opt-out. Technical logs are retained for the period needed for security and troubleshooting. When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or anonymised; backups are removed through their normal retention cycle. Contact us for the retention arrangements applicable to your records.

11 Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls and safeguards within our service providers. Access should be limited to people who need it for their role. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your login details confidential and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

12 Your rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of processing or a portable copy of information you provided. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and may object to direct marketing at any time. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions, including the protection of other people's rights and legally privileged information. To make a request, use enquiries@fcos.co.uk. We may need information to verify your identity and will respond within the applicable statutory time limit, normally one month, explaining any lawful extension or other qualification.

You may raise a privacy complaint with us at the same address. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ or by telephone on 0303 123 1113. You do not have to complain to us first.

13 Children and changes to this policy

HiLex individual membership is intended for adults. If an adult's enquiry includes information about a child, that information is handled only as necessary for the enquiry or relevant legal service, with appropriate safeguards.

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the service or legal requirements. The current version is available on our website, with its update date. We will draw material changes to your attention where appropriate and seek fresh consent where legally required.
